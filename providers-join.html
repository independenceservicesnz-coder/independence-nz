Common questions

Do I pay commission on bookings? ▼
No — never. You pay a flat $99/month subscription and keep 100% of every service payment. Independence NZ does not take any percentage of your earnings.
How does payment work? ▼
Customers pay through the Independence NZ platform. Once a booking is completed, payment is released to you within 2 business days via direct bank transfer.
Can I cancel my subscription? ▼
Yes. You can cancel anytime with no penalty. Your listing will remain active until the end of your current billing period.
What's the vetting process? ▼
We verify your business registration, insurance, and conduct a police background check. This is what makes our platform trusted by customers and their families.
Is there a free trial? ▼
Yes — approved providers get 30 days free to try the platform before their first billing cycle begins.
